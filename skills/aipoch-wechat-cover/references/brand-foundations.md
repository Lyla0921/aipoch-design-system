# AIPOCH Brand Foundations For WeChat Covers

## Use These Cues

- **Highlight:** `#F1DD67`; use it for a highlight, label, underline, or small focal surface—not as a default full background.
- **Ink:** `#111111`; use it for primary text and graphic structure.
- **Canvas:** `#FFFFFF`; **surface:** `#F7F7F4`; **line:** `#E7E5DE`; **muted:** `#6B6B66`.
- **Supporting accents:** `#EA580C`, `#3E7A63`, `#A9772A`, and `#4B6778`; keep them subordinate to ink and highlight.

Use a calm, precise, technical visual character. Favour clear hierarchy, generous negative space, thin structural lines, deliberate crops, and one focal idea. Avoid decorative card grids, glossy 3D blobs, excessive yellow, busy gradients, generic stock-business imagery, and ornamental frames.

## Typography

- Use **Inter** for Latin display and body text.
- Use **PingFang SC**, **Microsoft YaHei**, or **Noto Sans CJK SC** for Chinese text where available.
- Use **Roboto Mono** only for short labels, dates, issue numbers, or metadata.
- Use **Tinos Italic** for at most one brief English emphasis, never for Chinese body copy.

Keep the title short and strong. Use one emphasis treatment only. Never use small, tightly packed text as the main message.

## Logo

Use `assets/aipoch-logo-primary.svg` on light surfaces and `assets/aipoch-logo-inverse.svg` on dark surfaces. Preserve proportions and clear space equal to the rendered logo height. Do not recolor, stretch, outline, shadow, or decorate the logo.

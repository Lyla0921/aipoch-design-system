# AIPOCH Collaboration And Workshop Variant

Use this variant only when the user explicitly identifies a partner, co-host, joint event, or official partner logo. It extends the core AIPOCH system; it does not replace the core logo, color, content-fidelity, or delivery rules.

## Co-Branded Header

- Build one left-aligned lockup: `AIPOCH logo + thin neutral divider + partner logo`.
- Use the supplied official partner asset. Do not redraw it, recolor it, alter its aspect ratio, or substitute typed text for the mark.
- Balance logos by optical height and visual weight, not equal width. Long horizontal partner marks may be wider while remaining visually equal in prominence.
- Center both marks within the same `36px` logo band at 1920 × 1080. Use a `1px` divider approximately `22px` high and a compact `12–20px` gap on each side.
- Preserve at least half the rendered logo height as surrounding clear space within the header metadata area.
- Keep the lockup position, scale, and divider identical across all content slides. Do not omit the partner logo on interior slides.
- Keep the section label at top right. It must not compete with or duplicate the partner lockup.
- A simplified cover or closing header is allowed only when the user asks for it; otherwise retain the same co-branded lockup.

## Flat Technical Surface

The collaboration-workshop variant uses a restrained warm technical palette while preserving the core AIPOCH brand tokens:

- Canvas: `#F4F1EC`
- Opaque panel surface: `#F8F6F1`
- Workshop accent: `#F6CC46`
- Subtle grid line: `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.035)`
- Header rule: `rgba(17, 18, 16, 0.28)`

These are variant tokens, not replacements for the core Design System colors.

Use the technical grid on content slides only unless the user requests it on the cover or closing page. At 1920 × 1080, the established workshop rhythm is `40px` horizontal by `43px` vertical with a `9px` vertical offset. Align the header rule at `y = 88px` to the nearest grid row so the rule and grid do not look doubled or misregistered.

Foreground panels must be solid and opaque. Use square corners, no border, and no shadow. The grid must stop visually at every panel boundary and may not show through diagrams, portraits, or summary art.

## Cover And Hero Media

- Keep the supplied title, subtitle, speaker or role, and date visible in the main composition. Header or footer metadata does not replace them.
- Preserve required capitalization and phrase-aware line breaks.
- If the right-side hero is a raster image, match its background matte exactly to the cover canvas.
- Avoid a visible rectangular layer, cropped fragment, or empty bottom strip. Extend, crop, or position the hero so its intended visual mass reaches the composition edge cleanly.
- Keep the hero stable; animate only semantic pixels, scan fragments, yellow frames, or internal analysis details.

## Partner And Team Imagery

- Normalize circular or masked portraits by apparent head size, not raw source dimensions.
- Match eye line, headroom, face scale, and shoulder exposure across peers.
- Check every crop individually: no clipped crown, incomplete face, or accidental source-background edge.
- Use an opaque surface behind illustrations and portraits whenever transparency would reveal the grid or unrelated elements.

## Motion For Workshop Visuals

- Use motion to explain research flow, evidence transfer, execution, or traceability.
- For line-based workflows, animate the connector itself or move one yellow signal continuously from left to right along the true path.
- Keep the signal out of unrelated card interiors and make the loop reset invisible.
- For rotating rings or rays, rotate only the geometry around its true center. Keep the statement in a separate centered layer so the text remains exactly centered and unobstructed.
- Ensure all diagrams are complete in their static and reduced-motion states.

## Review Checklist

- Both official logos appear in the header and are optically balanced.
- The divider, header rule, grid row, and top alignment are consistent on every applicable slide.
- The grid does not penetrate foreground panels.
- No panel uses a shadow, outline, or unintended rounded corner.
- Cover title, subtitle, speaker or role, and date are all present when supplied.
- Mixed-language capitalization and intentional line breaks are preserved.
- Portraits have comparable head size and no clipped crowns.
- Animated paths follow real connectors; rotating geometry does not move or cover its centered text.

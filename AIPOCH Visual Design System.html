AIPOCH / Design System

AIPOCH Design System

Updated 2026-10-15aipoch.com ↗

AIPOCH’s design system provides a shared visual foundation for our website, social media, and in-product motion. It brings together typography, color, brand assets, and reusable components to keep these experiences consistent.

The system also supports branded HTML presentations and physical materials, including T-shirts, handheld signs, and bags. GitHub-hosted skills for creating brand visuals and HTML presentations will be added as the resource library grows.

Naming & Concept

AIPOCH brings together “AI” and “epoch,” expressing our vision of a new era of scientific discovery powered by artificial intelligence. Always write AIPOCH as one word in uppercase, without spaces or hyphens.

Visual Concept

The circular base, central space, and radial connections of the product-system illustration become a language of rings, connections, and dots.

These supporting graphics are primarily used across physical brand materials and merchandise, including T-shirts, bags, handheld signs, and stickers.

These forms suggest an intelligent core within a connected, evolving system.

Clear Space

Define `x` as the full rendered height of the logo. Maintain a minimum clear space of `1x` on all sides, so no text, image, or UI element enters that area.

In this diagram, the logo is rendered at `32px` tall, so each clear-space margin is also `32px`. In constrained UI, clear space must reduce to `0.5x` only when necessary. The clear space always scales with the logo size.

Minimum Size

The current website navigation uses the horizontal logo at approximately `32px` tall. Treat `32px` as both the minimum and the standard UI size, then scale up for larger interface contexts.

Long copy

Keep the research context visible so readers can understand the evidence and decide what to do next.

Use left-aligned text with a natural ragged right edge, never justified text. Aim for 45–75 characters per line where space allows; adjust the measure to avoid a lone final word.

Do not truncate essential research, safety, score, form-error, or handoff copy. Ellipsis is reserved for preview cards explicitly designed for it.
